# NOAA GOES Satellite Images

Do NOAA's full-disk images show the aurora? No. For a few reasons.

## Details

- NOAA's GOES-18 and GOES-19 geostationary satellites show full-disk renders of the earth, mostly focusing on clouds and other weather data.
- Turns out, those satellites don't really have imagers at the wavelengths that show in auroral emmissions.
